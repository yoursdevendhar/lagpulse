Reading the numbers

Loosely based on thresholds that matter for real-time traffic.

< 40 ms

Latency is comfortable for calls and competitive games. Above 120 ms, reactions start to feel delayed.

< 10 ms

Jitter under 10 ms keeps audio smooth. Spikes above 20 ms often sound like clipping or robotic voices.

0%

Packet loss should stay at zero. Even 1–2% can cause rubber-banding because TCP retransmits stall the stream.
